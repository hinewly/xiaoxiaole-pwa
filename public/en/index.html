Swap two neighboring fruits — line up 3 or more of the same to pop them!

How to play

  • Tap two neighboring fruits to swap them (dragging works too). Line up 3 or more of the same fruit to pop them and score.
  • If a swap makes no match, the fruits swap back — no penalty.
  • After a pop, fruits fall down to fill gaps — chain combos (Combo) multiply your score.
  • If no matches are possible, the board shuffles automatically and the game continues.
  • Your progress is saved on this device automatically.
